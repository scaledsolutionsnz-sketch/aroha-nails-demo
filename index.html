My story

From nail table to treatment table.

Tracey trained as a nail technician back in 2003. She moved to Te Aroha in 2019, could not find a salon to do her own nails, and Aroha Nails was born. As clients asked for waxing and tinting, the salon grew with them.

Then a Reflexology introduction course changed the direction of the whole business. She was hooked, qualified, and Aroha Nails and Beauty was reborn as Aroha Nails and Reflexology, a safe and completely natural way of supporting the body to heal itself. Treatments run from a private, peaceful salon at her home on Mountain View Road, with mobile visits for clients who cannot travel.

The healing side

What is reflexology?

An ancient form of healing dating back over 5000 years, applied to the feet, hands or ears. Gentle, mindful pressure relaxes the body and mind, restores calm to the nervous system and supports the body to return to balance and heal itself.

Reflexology can support

  • Anxiety, depression, brain function and memory
  • Pain management and nerve function
  • Headaches, migraines and stress
  • Better sleep, energy and mood
  • Digestion, circulation and lymphatic drainage
  • Immunity, hormonal imbalances and fertility

And ear seeding?

Ear seeding is acupuncture without the needles, a non-invasive form of healing from Traditional Chinese Medicine. Tiny plant seeds on plasters sit on reflex points of the ear, stimulating nerve endings close to the central nervous system, so the effect is quick and keeps working between visits.

Clients book ear seeds for stress and anxiety relief, pain, insomnia, weight management and cravings, digestion and immune health.

Reflexology is a complementary therapy, designed to work alongside standard medical care rather than replace it.
