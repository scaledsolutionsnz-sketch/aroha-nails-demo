What you can book in

Everything is done in one private room, one appointment at a time, so nothing gets rushed to fit the next person in.

01

Dip powder nails

The studio speciality. Colour is layered into a powder rather than cured under a lamp, so the finish is hard wearing and sits light on your own nail.

02

Acrylic & gel extensions

Added length in whatever shape suits your hands. Almond, coffin, square or a soft oval, filed and balanced so they wear evenly.

03

Manicures & gel colour

Shape corrected, cuticles tidied, then polish or gel on top. Chip free for a few weeks with none of the grow out line you get from a rushed set.

04

Nail art

Freehand detail, chrome, foils and hand painted work. Bring a photo of what you have seen, or leave the design with Tracey.

05

Pedicures

Soak, file, cuticle work and colour, plain or with gel. Men's manicures and pedicures are on the books here too, no fuss about it.

06

Waxing, massage & reflexology

Brows, lip and chin, body waxing, massage and reflexology. Book one on its own, or add it to a nail appointment and get it all done in the one trip.

The studio

A private room, one client at a time.

Aroha Nails is a small studio on Mountain View Road in Te Aroha, run by Tracey, a qualified nail technician and beauty therapist. It is quiet, it is private, and the door is not swinging open with walk ins while you are trying to relax.

Appointments run long enough to do the prep properly, because that is what decides whether a set lasts three weeks or three days. If you are not sure what you want, bring photos and we will work it out in the chair.

  • Qualified nail technician and beauty therapist
  • One private room, by appointment, never a queue
  • Late nights Tuesday to Thursday and Saturday mornings
