1. About these terms

These terms cover appointments and services provided by Aroha Nails at 6 Mountain View Road, Te Aroha 3320. By booking an appointment you agree to the terms set out on this page. The wording here is placeholder content prepared for a website demonstration and should be reviewed before the site goes live.

2. Bookings

Appointments are made by phone, text or email. A booking is confirmed once the studio has replied with a day and time. Please arrive at the time booked. Arriving late may mean the treatment is shortened so the following client is not held up.

3. Cancellations and no shows

  • Please give as much notice as you can if you need to change or cancel an appointment.
  • Repeated late cancellations or missed appointments may mean a deposit is asked for on future bookings.
  • If the studio needs to move your appointment, you will be contacted as early as possible and offered the next available time.

4. Prices and payment

Prices are confirmed at the time of booking and can vary with the length of the appointment, the service chosen and the condition of your natural nails. Payment is due at the end of your appointment unless something different has been agreed beforehand.

5. Aftercare and guarantees

Nail work is looked after best with sensible aftercare, which will be explained to you in the studio. If something lifts, chips or breaks within a few days of your appointment, get in touch and it will be looked at. Damage caused by knocks, picking, or removing a set at home is not covered.

6. Health, allergies and suitability

  • Please mention any allergies, skin conditions, nail infections, medications or pregnancy before your appointment so the right products and treatments can be chosen.
  • Some treatments may not be suitable in certain circumstances, and the studio may decline or adjust a service on that basis.
  • Please reschedule if you are unwell on the day.

7. Children and guests

The studio is a small private room. Please let the studio know when booking if you plan to bring a child or a guest with you, so there is space and time to look after everyone comfortably.

8. Liability

Services are carried out with reasonable care and skill. To the extent permitted by New Zealand law, including the Consumer Guarantees Act 1993, the studio is not liable for indirect or consequential loss. Nothing in these terms limits rights you have that cannot be excluded by law.

9. Website content

Images on this website include stock photography used to show styles and treatments. Reviews shown on the homepage are taken from the studio's public Google listing and are reproduced in the reviewers' own words. Information on the site is provided in good faith and may change without notice.

10. Contact
