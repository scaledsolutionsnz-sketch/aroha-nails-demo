Privacy Policy

What Aroha Nails collects, why it is collected, and how it is looked after.

Placeholder content for this demo

1. Who this covers

This policy applies to Aroha Nails, a nail and beauty studio at 6 Mountain View Road, Te Aroha 3320. It explains how personal information is handled under the Privacy Act 2020. The wording here is placeholder content prepared for a website demonstration and should be reviewed before the site goes live.

2. What is collected

  • Your name, phone number and email address, so appointments can be booked and confirmed.
  • Notes relevant to your treatments, such as allergies, skin sensitivities or the products used on your last visit.
  • Appointment history, so the studio knows what was done and when.

3. How it is collected

Information is given directly by you, usually when you call, text or email to make a booking, or when you talk through a treatment in the studio. This website does not include any contact form, and nothing is collected automatically from you when you browse these pages beyond standard hosting logs.

4. How it is used

  • Making, confirming and rescheduling your appointments.
  • Choosing products and treatments that suit you.
  • Getting in touch about a booking, or replying to a question you have asked.

Your details are not sold, rented or traded, and are not used for marketing lists.

5. Who it is shared with

Client information stays with the studio. It is only shared where you have asked for it, or where the law requires it. Third party services used to run the site, such as web hosting or the embedded Google map, are operated by their own providers under their own privacy terms.

6. Storage and security

Booking and treatment records are kept securely and only for as long as there is a good reason to keep them. Access is limited to the studio owner.

7. Cookies and analytics

This site does not set advertising or tracking cookies. Embedded content, such as the Google map near the bottom of the homepage and fonts served by Google, may set cookies controlled by those providers.

8. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the personal information the studio holds about you, and ask for it to be corrected if it is wrong. Requests can be made using the contact details below.

9. Reviews and photos

Photos of nail work are only shared publicly with your permission. Reviews shown on this website are drawn from the studio's public Google listing, where the reviewer has already chosen to publish them.

10. Contact

For anything to do with privacy, call the studio on 021 343 445 or send an email.
